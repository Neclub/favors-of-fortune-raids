Tier 2 – Plane of Greed Raid 2 – Taurlton, The Doorman
Raid Giver: Quisl'ing The Trustworthy
Requirements: Level 130
Background
Taurlton, the Doorman, was ordered to not let anyone into the palace unless they are on the list and you are not on the list.
Encounter Overview
The Doorman is a four stage encounter that takes place in one of the upper levels of the city. Taurlton may be recently promoted, but he has a knack for crushing things smaller than him and he is a very large minotaur. The Doorman plans to use his size, immense strength, and horns to smash, crush, and bleed you out.
• Minotaur: Taurlton is like a bull, ready to dig his horns into anything or anyone that dares to stand up to him
• Bouncer: Due to his immense size, Taurlton found a lot of work as a bouncer, tossing upstarts to the back of the line and black listing patrons that got out of hand
• Has a List: To be clear, the list is empty, but you are for sure not on it
• Doesn't Work Alone: Just because he is the biggest and potentially the strongest there is doesn't mean he works alone
This is a 54-player raid for groups with gear from the latest expansion.
Stage 1 (100% - 85%)
Overview
Taurlton takes his job very seriously and when he says you aren't on the list, he means it. He will utilize gore attacks, bleeds, and literal chunks of the city to drive you off.
Mechanics
Taurlton
Not on the List
• Occurs every 45 seconds
• Gores the main tank
◦ Deals high damage
◦ Causing a stack of Sucking Wound
Sucking Wound
• DoT effect that stacks up to 4 times
• Each stack refreshes the timer and deals increasingly more damage
• At 4 stacks the target Bleeds Out
Tossing the Trash
• Occurs every 11% health
• AoE Cone that must be soaked by at least 10 players
• Player hit will take damage and receive a stack of Sucking Wound
• If not enough player soak then the entire raid receives a stack of Sucking Wound
Strategy: Soak the AoEs, watch your stacks, and keep moving forward.
Stage 2 (84% - 65%)
Overview
You haven't gotten the message yet, but that's okay, Taurlton is calling in some assistance to help squash the upstarts and he will toss chunks of rubble into the air to rain down on your heads for good measure.
Mechanics
Taurlton
Not on the List
• Continues
Sucking Wound
• Continues
Tossing the Trash
• Continues
Falling Debris
• Occurs every 90 seconds
• 5 rubble piles will fall from the sky
• Players that step into the rubble piles will take damage and destroy the rubble pile
Hulking Bruiser
• Spawns ever 13% health
• Jump into the arena, crashing down on targeted player's heads
• Deal damage to anyone around the targeted player
• If two bruisers get too close to one another they become emboldened
◦ Increasing attack speed and providing a damage shield
Denizens
Your fighting has drawn the attention of opportunistic denizens who are looking for their chance to get ahead
• Occurs every 110 seconds
• Random assortment of adds spawn from the edge of the arena
◦ Made Centaur - mezzable
◦ Enraged Henchman - charmable
◦ Desperate Opportunist - rootable
Strategy: Watch your feet and don't step in the rubble, you may need them later. Control small adds and tackle the bigger ones.

Achievements
Complete additional challenges for special recognition:
1. Threat Escalation: Kill 6 enraged bruisers
2. Threat Mitigation: Never let a bruiser get enraged
3. Reserve List: Never destroy a debris pile without being on the black list
