Tier 3 – Plane of Guile Raid 1 – Chronicles of War
Raid Giver: Tovigor Nolg
Requirements: Level 130
Background
The Custodian of Knowledge, the Scribe of Guile, the Record Keeper, all names given to the being that knows everything about every battle ever fought and he is standing between you and the Lord of Guile.
Encounter Overview
The Record Keeper has spent his long existence watching others fight and that isn't about to change. He will not be engaging you directly, instead he will summon echoes of past battles that fight in his stead. The fight will take place over three stages, each stage adding another echo from the past to the mix. Drawing the echoes from the past does require effort and defeating them enacts a toll on the Record Keeper.
• Echoes of the Past: The Record Keeper will not fight you directly, instead you will face off against echoes from past battles. These echoes are fragments or snapshots of those encounters
• Wombo Combo: Each echo has their own set of abilities and some combinations may be more difficult than others
• Library: The 'arena' is as much as part of this encounter as the echoes themselves. Limited space will make you think fast on your feet when adds, echoes, and abilities start occurring
This is a 54-player raid for groups with gear from the latest expansion.
Stage 1 (One Echo)
Overview
The Record Keeper has watched your battles, but wants to witness your strength first hand. To test the waters he will be summoning a single echo.
Mechanics
Record Keeper
Summon Echo of Past Battles
• One of Six echoes will be summoned
◦ The echo is random and will change every attempt
• Once the echo is defeated the encounter will progress to stage 2
Books
• Spawn every 60 seconds*
◦ Only spawn if the echo does not have adds associated with it
• 3 books spawn at a time
Paper Cyclones
• Spawn every 145 seconds*
◦ Only spawn if the echo does not have adds associated with it
• 4 cyclones spawn at a time
• Relatively low health adds that explode, dealing damage to the raid, when killed
• Cyclones can be mezzed
◦ If mezzed they will not explode
Strategy: Fight the echo, eliminate books, and be careful not to accidently destroy the cyclones until you are ready.
Stage 2 (Two Echoes)
Overview
Things are getting interesting, the Record Keeper wants to make you sweat, so he is summoning two echoes.
Mechanics
Record Keeper
Summon Echoes of Past Battles
• Two of Six echoes will be summoned
◦ The echoes are random and will change every attempt
◦ The same echo from stage 1 will not be summoned
• Once the echoes are defeated the encounter will progress to stage 3
Books
• Continue to spawn
Paper Cyclones
• Continue to spawn

Stage 3 (70% - 0%)
Overview
The Record Keeper may have underestimated you, but he has a plan. What if you had to deal with three echoes at all times? That will surely defeat you.
Mechanics
Record Keeper
Summon Echoes of Past Battles
• Three of Six echoes will be summoned
◦ The echoes are random and will change every attempt
• When an echo is defeated it will cause feedback on the Record Keeper, dealing 10% of his health
• There will be three echoes up at all times, when one is defeated another will spawn
◦ No two of the same echo can be up at the same time
Books
• Continue to spawn
Paper Cyclones
• Continue to spawn
Strategy: Three echoes will always be up during this stage, eliminate the more difficult ones quickly.
