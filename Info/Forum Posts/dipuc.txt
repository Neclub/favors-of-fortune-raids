Tier 1 – Plane of Desire Raid 2 – Dipuc, General of Scorn
Raid Giver: Uldrek
Requirements: Level 130
Background
Initially ordered by Scorn, Queen of Thorns, to take control of the Plane of Desire, General Dipuc was aggressive and methodical, but further destabilized the realm. New orders have been given, but the general is ignoring them and appears to have gone rogue.
Encounter Overview
Dipuc is a three stage encounter the revolves around dodging attacks, destroying statues with boss mechanics before they become active, and coordinating the placement of auras.
• Master Archer: Much like his queen, Dipuc is a master with the bow
• Statues: Scorn's influence is seeping into the very fabric of realm and bringing it to life
• General: Every general commands troops
This is a 54-player raid for groups with gear from the latest expansion.
Stage 1 (100% - 76%)
Overview
Dipuc, overconfident he can handle the situation, fights alone, relying on his skill with the bow to vanquish the foes in front of him.
Mechanics
Dipuc
Piercing Shot:
• Occurs every 60 seconds
• Teleports to a location
• Fires a piercing shot that must be avoided
Strategy: Mechanics light stage. Burn the boss and avoid the line.
Stage 2 (75% - 56%)
Overview
Taking the fight more seriously Dipuc digs deeper into his arsenal and calls on the aid of his soldiers.
Mechanics
Dipuc
Piercing Shot:
• Continues
Shatter Shot:
• Occurs every 100 seconds
• Targets a player with a cluster of arrows
• When the arrows impact they splinter and fire off in different directions
• Will destroy the statues around the room
Scornful Touch
• Occurs every 245 seconds
• Targets a charmable add and enrages it
Scorn-touched Satyrs and Scornful Dryads
The general calls on additional support. Some of them can be controlled while others cannot.
Strategy: Short stage, so control damage. Continue to avoid the piercing shot, move the shatter shot away from the raid, and handle the adds that spawn.
Stage 3 (55% - 0%)
Overview
General Dipuc is no longer taking it easy, he is now throwing everything at the players, including curses and additional adds.
Mechanics
Dipuc
Piercing Shot:
• Continues
Shatter Shot:
• Continues
Scornful Touch
• Continues
Curse of the Broken Heart
• Occurs every 7% health
• Picks 3 random players
• After a short delay players will create an area around them
• Anyone caught in the area will suffer dmg and have their attack speed and casting slowed
• Creates Unrequited Love zones
◦ If two zones overlap they will detonate, dealing significant dmg to the raid
Scorn-touched Satyrs and Scornful Dryads
Continue to aid the general
Lifelike Masterpiece
Remaining statues around the room become infused with scorn and come to life, attacking the general's enemies
Strategy: Control tougher lifelike statues, do not stack the curse auras and continue to execute the previous stage's mechanics. Keep in mind, space is limited.
Achievements
Complete additional challenges for special recognition:
1. Shattered Art: Destroy None of the Statues
2. Devastating Love: Detonate Three Unrequited Love zones
3. Love Lost: Do not detonate any Unrequited Love zones
